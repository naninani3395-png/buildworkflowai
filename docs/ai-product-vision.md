# BuildFlow AI Product Vision

BuildFlow AI is an original AI-powered construction ERP and project management platform.

## Core Promise
- Help construction teams understand delays, costs, workload, and inventory through AI.
- Turn project data into concise recommendations and executive summaries.
- Provide a modern SaaS experience with dashboards, project modules, and AI copilots.

## AI Use Cases
- Show delayed projects
- Identify the employee with the highest workload
- Generate monthly financial summaries
- Recommend inventory reorder actions
- Summarize project health for leadership

## Future Evolution
- Gemini integration with real backend data
- Multi-company and role-based workflows
- Notifications, reports, document analysis, and voice assistant features
